Contact Index Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

What the app accesses

Contact Index accesses contact names, phone numbers, and organization names from your Android address book only after you grant the READ_CONTACTS permission. This access is required to build the app's local contact index.

Storage and sharing

The index is stored locally on your device in the app's private database. Contact data is not sent to our servers, cloud services, analytics, advertising networks, or third parties. The app contains no account or cloud synchronization.

Backups

If you choose to create a backup, the app creates an unencrypted ZIP file containing JSON, CSV, and VCF copies and saves it to the location you select. Anyone who obtains that file can read its contents. Restore reads a ZIP file selected by you and replaces the local index.

External actions

A phone call or WhatsApp conversation is opened only when you tap the corresponding action. Those actions are handled by the relevant Android or third-party application under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke contact permission in Android settings. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. You can delete exported backups using your device's file manager.
